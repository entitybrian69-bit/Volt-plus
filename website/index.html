Features & Capabilities

Everything built into Volt launcher plus for mobile Java Edition.

Vulkan & GLES 3.0 Pipeline

Uncapped up to 260 FPS, VSync battery mode, 25–100% resolution scaling, cave culling, and fast chunk uploads.

31 Native .hshader Packs

Built-in Horizon Native Shaders plus Modrinth shader search with volumetric clouds, sun shafts, and water reflections.

260 Mods & CurseForge Worlds

One-tap install for 260 Horizon .hmod mods, Modrinth resource packs, CurseForge worlds, and custom .hmod HTTPS imports.

Control Hub & Layout Editor

4 control presets including MCPE Bedrock & GoyControl, dynamic floating joystick, per-button scaling, and gyroscope aiming.

Replay Studio & MP4 Export

Smooth timeline scrubbing, keyframe camera paths, and offline hardware H.264 MP4 rendering directly into Movies/VoltPlus.

Screen Recorder up to 165 FPS

Record 240p–1440p gameplay with microphone, in-game, or silent audio and optional HUD hiding.

Content Library & Full SAF

Built-in File Manager and full Android Storage Access Framework read/write support for mods, packs, worlds, and controls.

Skins Studio, LAN & Updater

3D Skins & Capes Studio, Java 26.3 servers & LAN multiplayer, Discord Rich Presence, and in-app Release updates.
